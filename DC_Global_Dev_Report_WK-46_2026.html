Week 46 (Nov 9-15, 2026) -- week-exclusive edition: 51 developments, all published this week. Nothing is carried over from earlier weeks and issues resolved since are left out. Risk dominated the week. In the US, residents filed a civil RICO suit against OpenAI and Effingham County officials over Project Camellia. PJM's market monitor put data centre load at $29.4bn of added capacity costs. Henderson NC, Edmond OK and Center Point IA passed moratoria. SpaceX plans a $24bn Texas data centre on land de-annexed to sidestep a moratorium. In Europe, Statnett paused new DC grid reservations in Norway, thousands protested in Milan, and Norway plans DC licensing. In the UK, Google blamed a PLC misconfiguration for its London-zone outage, the Welsh Government called in the Cardiff East plan, and 6,000+ marched in Edinburgh. In Australia, NEXTDC halted uncontracted projects and the 1.2 GW Tungkillo hub was paused. Qatar: Syntys (Ooredoo) broke ground on a 20 MW AI/HPC data centre. In the GCC, HUMAIN signed SAR2.5bn of GPU and grid financing, DAMAC targets a 10 GW pipeline, and Oman plans a 400 MW Salalah campus. Growth continued: CoreWeave posted $3.66bn revenue on a ~$132.5bn backlog, SoftBank, Grab and PETROS committed $1.85bn of seed capital in Malaysia, Equinix opened SG6, Kraft proposed 900 MW in Massachusetts, and Egypt signed a $750M Forbes Tower data centre.
