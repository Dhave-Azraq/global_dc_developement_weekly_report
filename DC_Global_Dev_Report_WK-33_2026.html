Week 33 lands the year's second state-level DC grid shock and the largest single Qatar DC financing to date. On August 3 Texas Governor Greg Abbott PAUSED data center grid connections across Texas -- JEPIC-USA and trade press estimate the pause puts approximately 20% of the total US DC pipeline (~49.8 GW) at risk of delay, with potential DC revenue losses reaching approximately $8 billion by Q1 2027 assuming ~60% of delayed capacity is AI-related. The Texas action follows New York Governor Hochul's July 14 executive-order ban (WK-30), making Texas the second US state to constrain hyperscale DC development at the state level. In Qatar (per editorial direction), MEEZA secured an additional QAR 1.6 billion ($439 million) Commodity Murabaha facility from Dukhan Bank to fund M-Vault 6, 7 and 8 -- adding 44 MW and taking MEEZA's Qatar capacity beyond 60 MW total (TradeArabia, The Tech Capital).
