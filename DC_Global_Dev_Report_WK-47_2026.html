Week 47 (Nov 16-22, 2026) -- week-exclusive edition: 64 developments, all published this week. Nothing is carried over from earlier weeks and items resolved or reversed since are left out. Controls tightened. A US BIS rule (Nov 16) curbs remote access to advanced chips via overseas data centres and adds licence requirements for Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil. Spain now requires DCs over 10 MW to prove 80% hourly-matched renewables. Alberta tabled Bill 42 and New Brunswick adopted strict approval rules. In the US, McHenry County IL, Salt Lake City, Port Angeles WA and Hillsboro OR passed or extended moratoria. SpaceX's $50bn Cochise County AZ campus faces a Dec 9 hearing, and the Senate passed a bill making DCs pay their energy costs. In the UK, Chapelcross drew a record 10,000+ representations, Elsham won outline permission for up to 1 GW, and Pure DC and Iomart stake sales advanced. Qatar: MEEZA signed a QR100m+ sovereign cloud region deal. GCC: the first Data Center World Middle East met in Abu Dhabi and Jordan plans a 400 MW Al-Risha site. Asia: NEXTDC cut FY27 capex by A$980m, PDG added $210m for Tokyo and ReNew plans 1 GW in India. Africa: Teraco began receiving wheeled wind power, Cassava seeks $1bn and South Africa offered Eskom sites for AI data centres. NVIDIA reported $115.8bn in quarterly revenue.
