Week 48 (Nov 23-29, 2026) -- week-exclusive, PARTIAL edition: the week is still in progress, so this covers only 25 developments dated Nov 23-24. Nothing is carried over from earlier weeks and resolved items are left out. US local resistance led the week. A Virginia judge halted construction of Google's Botetourt County data centre over a FOIA breach. Aurora, CO passed a moratorium and Vantage dropped its Trident site. Augusta's Planning Commission recommended denying the 383-acre Nixon campus, and Shelby County, Piedmont, Salem OR, Madison WI and Yakima County all moved on new rules. Deals: KKR is reportedly in talks for EdgeCore at up to $10bn, and Cogent sold three DCs for $35M. In Europe, SpainDC is preparing a Supreme Court challenge to Spain's 80% hourly-renewables decree, and a 200 MW+ campus is planned in Salaspils, Latvia. In the UK, Drax raised guidance, citing its DC partnership. In Asia, Moonshot AI reportedly cancelled a ~$150M Indonesian contract as US chip licensing bites, CtrlS signed for 150 MW in Bihar, and Samsung is ramping AI SSD NAND. In Africa, CloudVantage launched Nigerian cloud regions. No new Qatar or GCC DC news was found for Nov 23-24.
