Week 45 (to Nov 4, including news from Oct 28 – Nov 1): US voters ruled on data centres directly, the UK set a Data Centre Covenant, and Qatari capital moved into UK data centres. On Nov 3, 41 DC ballot measures in 10 states went to voters: Trenton, Ohio banned data centres (~80%), blocking an Amazon annexation, central Ohio rejected DCs in all five races, Peru, Maine passed a 180-day moratorium and Mooresville NC a 12-month pause. In the UK, Drax, Brookfield and Radiant announced a £20bn+ GW-scale DC partnership at Drax Power Station, funded by the Brookfield–Qai JV (£2.5bn phase 1, up to 100 MW from 2028). The Budget's Data Centre Covenant offers strategic sites priority grid access. Telehouse closed a Docklands site for 65 MW, TPG is reportedly in talks for Ark at ~$5bn, and BBC found four UK DC plans weighing on-site gas. Qatar: Qai's Drax commitment is this week's Qatar DC story; no new in-country project was found. Hyperscaler earnings showed Microsoft capex below plan on late partner capacity and AWS capex at $77bn; AMD's Data Center revenue rose 97%. In Asia, Naver signed $9bn Brookfield financing for Sejong, Malaysia's PM said SoftBank plans $50bn, and Cortex DC targets 2 GW in India. In the GCC, MEED counts $93bn+ across 174 projects and Beyon is expanding green DCs in Bahrain. In Africa, Infragora's Nigerian DCs start in Q2 2027 and Egypt plans 5-7 mega DCs. Ahead: Qualcomm results Nov 4, HUMAIN Riyadh bids Nov 8, NVIDIA Nov 17, Microsoft Ignite Nov 17-20.
