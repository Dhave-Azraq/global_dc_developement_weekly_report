Week 36 opens with NVIDIA's Q2 FY27 blowout on August 26: revenue of $96.2 billion (+106% year-over-year, +18% quarter-over-quarter) beat the $92.07 billion consensus by a wide margin. Data Center revenue alone reached $89.0 billion (+117% YoY, 92.7% of total revenue), split between Hyperscale at $48.7 billion and ACIE (AI Clouds, Industrial, and Enterprise) at $40.3 billion. Non-GAAP EPS came in at $2.22 (GAAP $2.46) against a $2.09 consensus, with gross margins at 75.0% on both GAAP and non-GAAP measures. Q3 FY27 guidance of $108.0 billion crushed the street. Fortune's headline: "Nvidia doubles Q2 revenue to $96 billion and crushes estimates, as CEO Jensen Huang says demand is accelerating." The same day, Anthropic signed a $45 billion cloud deal with UK-based Nscale for approximately 460 megawatts of compute at Nscale's West Virginia data center over six years, running on Nvidia Vera Rubin chips, with the facility expected to come online at the end of 2027 (CNBC/FT/QZ/TechCrunch/Bloomberg). That follows Anthropic's July $5 billion AMD compute deal and $10 billion Volta (Norway) commitment. AMD officially launched its Helios rack-scale AI system: 72 Instinct MI455X GPUs plus 18 sixth-generation EPYC "Venice" CPUs, Pensando networking and ROCm software in an open rack-scale design, with analyst pricing estimates around $5.25 million per rack. Customer commitments accompanying the launch were substantial: Anthropic committed 2 gigawatts of Instinct GPUs in Helios racks with the first gigawatt deploying in H1 2027; Meta committed to 6 gigawatts across multiple generations with the first gigawatt shipping H2 2026; and Microsoft's Azure ND MI455X v7 SKU will begin shipping Helios-based systems in the second half of 2026 for frontier-model inference, Azure AI services and customer workloads. Windows Forum reports Helios has entered full production with MI455X rack shipments due Q3. On the demand side, Amazon separately committed to purchase 2 million Nvidia GPUs for AWS data-center buildout (Aug 28 DataCentreNews weekly). The Big Four hyperscalers' 2026 capex plan totals about $630 billion -- roughly 62% higher than 2025's record $388 billion -- and the narrative has explicitly shifted from aggregate capex to time-to-energy, network scale, and speed of converting infrastructure into revenue-generating tokens. On the market forecast side, Arizton via GlobeNewswire (Aug 31) published a Europe hyperscale outlook: $52.02 billion in 2025 rising to $139.90 billion by 2031 at 17.93% CAGR -- the third major hyperscale forecast in three weeks after WK-35's MarketsandMarkets ($608.54 billion global by 2030 at 24.6% CAGR) and WK-34's ResearchAndMarkets ($245 billion → $1.21 trillion by 2040 at 12.1% CAGR). On the risk side, BNEF calculates the Texas audit pause puts roughly 20% of the U.S. data-center pipeline at risk of delay (Utility Dive). Hill County Texas rescinded its local moratorium after a lawsuit and other Texas counties abandoned similar plans (MultiState/Enverus). Henry County Virginia adopted the strictest data-center zoning framework to date on Aug 25: rezoning plus special-use permit, 1,000-foot setbacks, a 50 dBA noise limit, and mandatory closed-loop cooling. U.S. grid interconnection waits now routinely stretch 4-5 years, and the construction industry faces a shortfall of about 439,000 workers. Despite the audit pause the pipeline continues to move: Google's Project Helium in Barber County Kansas will be heard at a special commissioners meeting on September 1 (multiple buildings, 500 full-time jobs); OpenAI announced Project Camellia in Effingham County Georgia as a long-term DC development; Kentucky PSC approved TeraWulf's 482 MW Justified Data Campus special power contract on Aug 28, clearing the WK-33 anchor; Microsoft's Vaasa Finland DC pre-construction works started (Aug 28), continuing the Nordic buildout. On the UK / Nscale side, the Anthropic $45 billion contract lands at Nscale's West Virginia site, complementing Nscale's $865 million North Carolina DC deal (DCK Investing), its new Nordscale Operations JV with Nordkraft to run the Narvik Norway DC, and its reported $3 billion US IPO filing (against the £14.6 billion March 2026 valuation baseline). Nscale's £2 billion Loughton Essex UK site remains grid-blocked for 2027 -- Data Centre Magazine ran a deep-dive titled "What Are the Factors Delaying Nscale's £2.6bn UK Investment?" AR Mason UK DC Intelligence continues weekly; 100+ UK projects remain on gas fallback. On the Qatar leg (per editorial direction), WK-35's MEEZA 2x-4x capacity plans (Mobile Europe: "almost double"; Telecompaper: "quadruple in 4 years") and Ooredoo/Syntys targeting 120 MW installed by 2030 (Fortune Aug 12) persist unchanged. Qatar's colocation stock stands at 13 operational facilities plus 5 upcoming, with Doha continuing to prominize as a Middle East regional hub -- MEEZA and Ooredoo lead the operator table. MEEZA's 2nd Dukhan Bank QAR 1.6 billion ($439 million) Sharia-compliant facility for M-Vault 6, 7 and 8 adds about 44 MW, taking MEEZA capacity beyond 60 MW. Ooredoo Group's QAR 2 billion landmark DC financing (WK-32) continues to fund the Doha corridor scale-up. Syntys operates its Nvidia Hopper-powered AI cloud locally in Doha for Ooredoo. Fortune's Aug 12 "Gulf's AI boom is driving the race to control the data highways" continues to name Qatar alongside Saudi HUMAIN and UAE G42 as the three central Gulf AI-infra players. Dr. Bader bin Dulaim (Dalham) Al-Fuhaid (Al-Fahad) Al-Hajri was appointed President of the Arab Federation for AI and Programming on August 17, 2026 at an extraordinary meeting of its Board of Directors (Gulf Times / Qatar Tribune / QNA). Next-phase agenda: developing a comprehensive strategic vision including training and research initiatives, programs to develop Arab talent, and preparations for launching joint projects with Arab universities and research centers. He named Qatar's advanced infrastructure and its technology and AI investments as the foundation on which the federation's programs and regional initiatives will build, and highlighted brain-drain reduction as a core priority. His international natural-resources firm board seats (per user brief) continue to reinforce Qatar's AI + resource strategic-investment posture -- now visible in MEEZA's 4-year quadrupling plan and Syntys's 120 MW 2030 target. AI DC Networking (Fortune / Semianalysis / Latitude Media): the story is now time-to-energy > total capex. Rubin ramp (Q3 FY27 guide $108B), Helios ramp, and custom-silicon TSMC Arizona are competing to convert power and land into revenue-generating tokens fastest. Hardware catalog persists: Qualcomm AI200 (2026 -- 768 GB LPDDR, 160 kW/rack DLC) + AI250 (2027 -- near-memory computing, >10x memory bandwidth); Bosch Rexroth Sytronix in Power & UPS (up to 20 dB(A) noise reduction); Bloom Energy SOFC Energy Server. AMD MI455X is now the AMD Helios first-launch SKU. Brockovich map refreshed Aug 31 (up from Aug 20); Facts & Myths, Noise Simulation, Community Photos and Communities & Legislation tabs live.
